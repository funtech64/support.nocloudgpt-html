====== Backups and Recovery ======
Your cloud account owner is responsible for preserving data. Before major updates, confirm that backups or Lightsail snapshots exist and that you can restore them. A snapshot is useful but is not a substitute for testing a restore.

Review whether your backup includes:
  * application data and chat history;
  * DokuWiki user content (outside the managed `support:` pages);
  * relevant service settings;
  * any external storage or database.

Keep secrets in your approved secret manager, not inside a wiki export. The support documentation ZIP includes only public help pages; it is **not** a full instance backup.

The documentation sync only replaces the managed `support:` namespace. It must not delete personal wiki pages or application data.
