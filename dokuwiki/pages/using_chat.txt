====== Using AI Chat ======
  - Open your instance's main HTTPS address.
  - Sign in.
  - Choose an installed model, if your interface shows a model selector.
  - Ask a short question to confirm the model responds.
  - Avoid pasting private customer data into a cloud-backed model unless authorized.

A **local model** runs using the computer's available resources. On small Lightsail instances it may be slow or unavailable. A **Jet / cloud-backed model** can be faster but uses outside services and Internet connectivity.

Chat history, document upload handling, model provider policies, and retention depend on your deployment and chosen features. Ask your installer to explain them before processing sensitive information.
