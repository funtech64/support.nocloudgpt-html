====== Getting Support ======
For official online help, visit [[https://support.nocloudgpt.com/|support.nocloudgpt.com]].

When contacting your installer or support representative, include:
  - Product line and software version.
  - Where the instance is hosted (for example AWS Lightsail / Ubuntu).
  - Feature affected and the page or step where it failed.
  - What you tried and any non-sensitive error text.

**Never include:** passwords, recovery codes, license keys, cloud account access keys, SSH private keys, authentication tokens, or unredacted personal/customer data.

If this local wiki is unavailable, open the online support center from another device.
